Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Overview

Buffington Family Medicine ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at buffingtonfamilymedicine.com.

This policy applies to information collected through our website only. For information about how we handle your protected health information (PHI), please refer to our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, available at our office.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use our contact form, you may provide us with:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message content

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Improve our website and services
  • Protect against spam and abuse (via Cloudflare Turnstile)

How We Protect Your Information

We implement security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages
  • CAPTCHA verification on forms to prevent abuse
  • Rate limiting on form submissions
  • Security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options)

Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — Website hosting, security, and performance. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — CAPTCHA verification on our contact form
  • Resend — Email delivery for contact form submissions
  • Google Maps — Embedded map on our contact page. Google Privacy Policy

Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics services. Cloudflare may set functional cookies necessary for security and performance.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any future communications

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
